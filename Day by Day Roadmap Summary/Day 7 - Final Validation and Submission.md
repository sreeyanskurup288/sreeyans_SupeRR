# Day 7 — Final Validation and Submission

## Objective
Stress-test the rankings, apply final availability updates, and prepare submission-ready materials.

## Tasks

1. Re-run Day 5 and Day 6 from the corrected data source.
2. Test eligibility thresholds and PPI sensitivity.
3. Check one-match dominance and rank stability.
4. Verify player names, roles, countries, exclusions, and ranking order.
5. Apply retention, availability, retirement, coaching-role, and other manual exclusions.
6. Generate final top-10 tables, charts, and methodology notes.
7. Confirm raw ZIP and CSV files are not committed.
8. Review the final README and submission checklist.

## Deliverables

- `scripts/day7_final_validation.py`.
- Final validation report under `outputs/day7/`.
- Ranking comparison chart.
- Final methodology and limitation notes.
- Submission checklist confirming repository cleanliness and reproducibility.

## Completion check

The final repository should contain only code, derived outputs, audits, charts, and documentation needed for evaluation—not the raw data pack.
